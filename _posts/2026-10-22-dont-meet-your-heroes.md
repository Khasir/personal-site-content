[Jacob Geller](https://youtube.com/@jacobgeller) is a video essayist on YouTube. He covers gaming, life, politics, and morality. He is a leftist. He is a Jew. He has committed sexual assault.

I [recently learned](https://youtu.be/05gdfFmEk3A) that Geller, one of the inspirations for this very blog, committed sexual assault over a decade ago. I, for one, feel conflicted. I’m a big fan of his work. But I’m disappointed in him. I’m sad this is how I and the rest of his audience have found out. And I’m sorry for the victim he hurt so long ago.

His most recent video (as of the time of writing) was on a number of games centred on infection, and infections as a metaphor for how games exploit the pleasure-seeking centre in our brains. Honestly, I don’t think it was his best work. Regardless, it’s unlikely I’ll watch his future videos, unless he addresses the elephant in the room. I don’t think I’d be able to watch without being reminded of all this.

You can read [his admission on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/jacobgeller.com/post/3mwtmr4ccas22). To be fair to him, he spoke with the victim the day after it happened and cooperated with the investigation, and he is respecting the victim’s right to anonymity. Of course, this is a one-sided admission due to the nature of the situation. Lots of rumours are flying around since this is the only statement that he has made.[^1] I think people are rightfully disappointed and angry for only finding this out now, and many want him to now face punishment to make up for the lack of it back then. Personally, I think losing the trust and admiration of his audience, and being [dropped by his platform partners](https://aftermath.site/jacob-geller-minnmax-allegations/) is punishment enough—I genuinely believe he’d never commit such a thing again.

But, you might say, what about justice for the nameless victim? Well, we don’t know what she wants. Burn him at the stake? Let him be? Luxuriate in his downfall? Without the victim stepping forward (which she doesn’t need to do), and without Geller adding any clarifying comments (out of respect for the victim), the vacuum gives space to the loudest to tear into Geller while his reputation crumbles.

I looked up to Geller. I still do, minus the assault. He seems genuinely introspective, thoughtful and empathetic. I understand the desire to atone for past mistakes by doing good. The whole debacle has me reflecting on my past mistakes. The people I’ve hurt unintentionally. The things I could have done better. I can only imagine what it’d be like to be assaulted. I do worry about myself, sometimes.

This news comes to me at a time when I’m feeling overwhelmed from volunteer obligations, work obligations, and [tense](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/fourth-arrest-bike-lane-removal-protest-toronto-9.7387669) [political events](https://www.cp24.com/local/toronto/2026/10/21/toronto-mayors-race-has-tightened-here-is-what-a-exclusive-poll-has-found/) I am invested in. Sometimes I wish I didn't care so much, or that I could just hole up and disappear.

If I were Geller, I might just delete the YouTube channel and pretend I never existed.

There was another essayist I used to watch, Exurb1a. I remember he used to move me to tears. But I stopped watching him when his ex called him out publicly for, you guessed it, sexual assault amongst other things. But unlike Geller, Exurb1a doesn’t seem to have even acknowledged the accusations over six years later. In retrospect, his writing did always seem to be somewhat haughty and un-emotional.

I used to listen to Video Games Hot Dog.[^2] It was made by the guys behind [Kingdom of Loathing](https://www.kingdomofloathing.com/login.php), but I listened because they discussed lots of different indie games that I otherwise would not have known about. They stopped suddenly in August of 2019, just before I began my masters at U of T. I found out what happened some time during the pandemic—Zack, who I thought of as the podcast lead, abused his ex-wife who came forward, and Kevin, who was quite mild-mannered, was often present and pretended not to notice when it happened. Again in retrospect, Zack seemed somewhat bro-ey and would often interrupt his fellow podcast hosts.[^3]

At this point, I should tell you that my last and only romantic relationship was with someone much younger than me. She was 20, I’m 29. If I’ve creeped you out, I don’t blame you. You have every right to think differently of me, and given what I’ve discussed above, I’d rather you know. But I will add that respect and consent were big parts of our relationship. For me, it wasn’t the age inasmuch as she was the first person to show a real interest in me. And for her, I think she found comfort in the idea of dating older, caring men. But I wouldn’t date that young again.

Anyways, I strongly considered disabling comments on this post and letting a vacuum form—this is my [parlour](/posts/cathedrals) after all.[^4] I still may in the future, 1) if comments get out of hand, and 2) because I don't want comfort or validation related to this post. Validation is my drug of choice—too much keeps me complacent. Besides, you shouldn’t meet your heroes.
