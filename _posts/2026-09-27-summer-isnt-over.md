## let's talk about the walrus in the room

{: style="max-width: 35rem"}
> *We ought to democratize our politics and our democracy, which are truly one and the same.*
> {: .large}
> 
> ---David Moscrop

My friend and fellow tech worker Jenny Zhang gave a talk as part of [Walrus Talks: The Billionaire Problem](https://thewalrus.ca/the-walrus-talks-the-billionaire-problem/). She signed up on Monday, I signed up on Tuesday, she wrote the talk on Wednesday, and I attended on Thursday. I caught up with some friends who happened to attend the talks too. Shoutouts to Poe for recognizing the back of my head and Samir for stopping at my row.

{% include figure.html src="/content/images/2026-09-24-walrus-talks-billionaire-problem.jpg" alt="Jennifer Hollett introduces The Walrus Talks: The Billionaire Problem." caption="*Walrus Talks: The Billionaire Problem.*" %}

Jenny’s speech was very inspirational. It instilled lots of pride in me for being a part of the Tech Workers Coalition. She vocalized a lot of the ideas and discussions that I’ve been privy to as part of TWC, but I’m sure was quite fresh for most of the audience. The idea of tech workers, unionizing? But why?!

The [recording is here](https://www.youtube.com/live/sqF4zSXr1IU?si=nw-X408DJQXmYV4a)—if you want to listen to her part, jump to [1:00:20](https://www.youtube.com/live/sqF4zSXr1IU?si=oVTHa3Sz-XykwIMo&t=3620), though I can recommend listening to the whole event since all the speakers gave excellent talks.

## so, when do we reach critical mass?

Friday’s Critical Mass bike protest had a huge turnout. My estimate is that at least a thousand people showed up to reclaim the streets. We started at High Park and went west till [Tom Riley Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UnuZ2FYizdtVk2P37), which is around the area where bike lanes are supposed to be ripped up in less than two weeks.

{% include figure.html src="/content/images/2026-09-25-critical-mass.jpg" alt="Hundreds of cyclists on Bloor as part of the Critical Mass bike protest during sunset on Sep 24, 2026. A sign prominently reads \"Save Bike Lanes\"." caption="*A huge turnout at Critical Mass. Not pictured: the remaining convoy of cyclists behind me.*" %}

During parts of the ride, I could feel myself getting emotional riding with the mass of cyclists. Joy at being part of something larger than myself. Frustration with those who swear and honk at our caravan passing by. Solidarity with those who cheer us on. Sadness at having to fight for our right to safety. All this because our beloved premier can’t get enough of driving and doesn’t care one bit about bikers.

The next day, my friend Shivi asked me what bike events I had been to recently. “I went to a bike protest last night,” I replied, as he showed me a Reddit post with my picture in it. Both of us were amazed—[I made the front page of r/Toronto](https://www.reddit.com/r/toronto/comments/1wqf5eo/critical_mass_sept_25/)![^1]

## my summer is only getting started

The aforementioned Samir had invited me to the [Second Summer music festival](https://secondsummerfest.com/) in the west end. I didn’t know any of the artists who’d be performing. But my policy is to say yes to most social invitations by default. We got a few of our other friends to sign up too.

{% include figure.html src="/content/images/NEW-SSF26-FULL-LINEUP-POSTER_1080X1350.jpg" alt="Poster for the Second Summer Festival, Sep 24--26 in Toronto's west end. Lists a large number of participating artists." caption="*The lineup for [Second Summer](https://secondsummerfest.com/).*" %}

What was interesting about this festival was how decentralized it was. It took place at multiple venues across the west end, so we walked and took the streetcar to a few different places. The first venue we attended was missing wristbands, so they drew S’s on us with permanent marker.

This is what I remember of the bands we listened to.

- [MEGGO](https://www.meggomusic.com/): Very soothing, relaxing.
- [Absolute Treat](https://absolutetreat.ca/): What a treat. Energetic. Samir did not, in fact, discover them from Heated Rivalry.
- [sundayclub](https://www.sundayclub.band/): Reminded me of a younger Paramore. Ears ringing.
- [shishi](https://shishiband.bandcamp.com/music): A song dedicated to the three worst people in the world: Putin, Trump and Netanyahu.
- [MIKE](https://mikelikesrap.bandcamp.com/): Funny and great stage presence even tho he was facing backwards for half the show. Got everyone to cheer every time he pumped his arm. Taught us a trademarked dance move for when you don't know what to do (raise your arms, turn your hands into fists, and wag your hands like paws).
- [W.I.T.C.H](https://w-i-t-c-h.bandcamp.com/): Incredible. Lots of energy for their age. [Zamrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamrock). A song about being careful who you choose as your lover, and playing games in the village as children under the moonlight. Song genres by purpose instead of style. Almost skipped a song but instead obeyed his white master. Songs that end three times.

I had a great time!
