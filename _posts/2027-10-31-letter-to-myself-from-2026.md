October 31, 2026

Hi Khasir,

Today is Halloween! I'm not planning to do anything in particular today, though I made some last-minute plans to meet up with A. I’m almost fully recovered from taking the flu and COVID shots two days ago (except I seem to be sweating profusely). Last night, I was playing some friendslop ([Moon Sweepers](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/)) with J and A. R was away since he’s moving this weekend. I was supposed to play [Mouthwashing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2475490/Mouthwashing/) for them but I was low energy from the shots.

- Did I beat Mouthwashing? (Yes, I hope so!)
- Is the Mouthwashing movie still being worked on? They [just announced](https://www.ign.com/articles/hit-indie-horror-game-mouthwashing-is-getting-a-live-action-movie-from-the-director-of-jennifers-body) it.
- Am I still a scaredy cat when I’m playing horror games? (Maybe less so?)

I’ve been feeling a bit overwhelmed lately. I think it’s due to having to deal with someone who was harassing me at [TfD](https://techfordemocracy.ca/) (which retriggered some of the negative memories with my ex) and the sudden [Waymo announcement](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/10/waymo-in-toronto/) from a few weeks ago. And being behind on uploading meetup recordings to the [CTTO YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CivicTechTO). Feeling like there’s so much I want to do, but so little time.

- Am I still stewarding TfD? (Probably? Despite my energy levels and inner protestations.) 
- Is TfD finally a non-profit? I was planning to have us incorporate by now, but it looks like it won’t happen this year. (Please say it’s a non-profit!)

I’ve been exploring my sexuality lately. Short-term flings, hookups. I want to explore while I still can, but honestly I’m also just afraid of being burned again in a serious relationship. Also, shocking how expensive [PrEP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-exposure_prophylaxis_for_HIV_prevention) is—Gilead really has the LBGT community in a chokehold.

- Am I dating anyone seriously now? (I hope so, for my own sake!)
- Is my bestie dating again too? (She deserves someone nice!)
- Is S still seeing V? (They seem to be getting along well!)

The first portion of the bike lanes in Etobicoke were [removed recently](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/etobicoke-bloor-street-west-bike-lane-removal-section-complete-9.7402894). The cycling community certainly made it difficult for Verdi Alliance, though. Unfortunately, I hadn’t been able to participate in the protests because I’ve somehow been busy almost every day for the last few weeks, and my commitment to keeping TfD running.

- Do we still have the bike lanes in Toronto? (I really, really hope so!)
- Has Mayor Chow done anything in her power to fight back? (I would think so? But as a creature of the province…)

I finally got rid of the ugly and uncomfortable couch that came with my unit—planning to order the new one today. I had been thinking of moving away, but decided not to yet (despite the lull in rent costs), instead hoping to freshen things up with new furniture. Besides, my contract ends in December and I’m not sure [CNO](https://nationalobserver.com/) will renew.

- Am I still living near Trinity Bellwoods? (Probably?)
- Am I still working for the National Observer? They do like me, but I think this hinges more on if they think I’m bringing in enough money. (Fingers crossed!)

Also, I recently (re)discovered [Remi Wolf](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC5jtIev24b3CWoUkCj1CIsg) and have been listening to her almost every day. I wanted to go to a Carly Rae Jepsen concert in May with S but all the tickets sold out on the first day of presale. :(

- How was the Remi Wolf concert?
- Did you land any CRJ tix??
- Are you still listening to Illenium? Said the Sky? Porter Robinson? Dabin? Any new artists?

Anyways, if you open this on Halloween 2027, you’ll be turning 31 in a few days. You’re getting old!

Just kidding. I know you (and your parents) place pressure on yourself for meeting the love of your life, getting your own place, having kids and living happily ever after. But it’s okay if you don’t do any of that. A life well-lived doesn’t need to follow a formula. Besides, you still have many years ahead of you.

Don’t be too harsh on yourself, okay?

—Khasir, 29 years old
